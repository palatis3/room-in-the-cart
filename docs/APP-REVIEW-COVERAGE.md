# Whole-app App Review coverage

**Submission status, November 20, 2026.** The iPhone Release configuration supports private shopping, dinners, pantry and records without signing in. A shopper can review a receipt locally, save a pack comparison and hand off a list to a retailer. Production household sign-in, guardian enrollment and the annual subscription are unavailable in this configuration. The public Support, Privacy and Terms URL settings are empty. The app and both extensions passed verification in the exported distribution-signed IPA. A new archive with the live production settings, working public pages and final App Store Connect disclosures remains necessary. [Signed IPA receipt](evidence/native/app-store-signing-20261120/verification.json) Apple’s guidelines were checked November 20 against the October 16 revision.
