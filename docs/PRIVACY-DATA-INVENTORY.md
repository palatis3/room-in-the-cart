# Privacy data inventory

Implementation inventory, 2026-11-03. This supports the native privacy manifest; it is not a published privacy policy or completed App Store privacy questionnaire. Reconcile it with the final submitted build and live provider settings before distribution.

The ordinary local Free workflow keeps its workspace on the iPhone. Signing into a household does not upload that existing workspace. Optional authenticated features transmit the specific account, household, request, shared-shop or billing data needed for those features. The manifest describes those optional collection paths even while email setup and real App Store product configuration remain deferred.

| Apple category | Implemented collection path | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Household member aliases, entered by people; `service/migrations/001_household_requests.sql` | Attribute household actions and requests |
| Email address | Supabase email authentication; `ios/Packages/HouseholdClient/Sources/HouseholdClient/AdultAuth.swift`, `service/src/browser-auth.ts` | Sign-in and account authentication |
| User ID | Verified provider subject, household member IDs and billing purchaser binding; household/member linkage for optional measurement consent and responses | App functionality for authorization, account records and membership; analytics for consented household outcome and retention measurement |
| Device ID | Enrolled device/session identifiers in the household service | Authenticate and revoke an enrolled device; not advertising |
| Other user content | Explicitly submitted household requests, shared shop content, instructions and proposed changes; newly reviewed optional household measurement response codes | App functionality for household collaboration; analytics for consented outcome, baseline and comparison responses |
| Other financial information | Reviewed shared-shop spending caps, quotes, shopper-entered charges, credits, holds, price observations and budget periods | Evaluate the shop budget, display the assigned shopper’s evidence and calculate authorized period totals |
| Purchase history | Verified Apple membership transaction/original-transaction identifiers and subscription state; existing verified Production subscription state matched with current measurement consent | App functionality to deliver, restore and revoke membership correctly; analytics for consented household purchase retention measurement |

All listed data is treated as linked to identity and used for app functionality, and none is used for tracking. User ID, Other user content and Purchase history additionally support optional household outcome and retention analytics. These declarations do not authorize new uploads. Existing authorization and explicit review boundaries still control each transmission.

The capture path processes speech and photo text on the iPhone. Speech capture keeps recognized text for review while microphone audio stays in transient buffers. A contributor can explicitly submit the reviewed request text. The spelling rule stays in the app’s protected local store. The app keeps local benchmarks and redacted diagnostic events on the device. Apple handles payment credentials and sends the app membership transaction evidence.

Optional spelling memory stores the original and corrected product-name fragments in backup-excluded storage. Private workspaces use their own local profile. An enrolled adult contributor uses a separate account, household member and language scope. Access changes hide the prior profile. Forget and opt-out remove the selected spellings, and confirmed account deletion clears the account’s spelling files across memberships and languages.

Optional scan evidence: the camera/photo route requires per-review on-device-processing consent. It stores reduced JPEG references (location metadata stripped), original text, explicit product matches and literal receipt annotations only in protected, backup-excluded app-private storage. The bounds are twelve sidecars per namespace and 2.1 MB per encoded record. Individual reference removal, recipe-photo removal, pickup deletion, and whole-ledger deletion retire the corresponding evidence; foreground tombstone replay retries cleanup and prevents late recreation. Typed recipes retain original text in the recipe without a media sidecar. Photo bytes are included only when the person explicitly selects source references for an encrypted owner recovery file in Records, reviews their size and private-content disclosure, and supplies a user-held recovery key. Readable and helper exports contain no attachment bytes; the app does not upload photos. Selected references are staged and validated with their records, then committed atomically as separate recovered evidence, without opening the original media namespace. Known source-reference removal markers scrub embedded evidence from earlier recovered copies while preserving their records and isolated workspaces, and prevent restoration through an older backup. Internal recovered media uses complete protection and is excluded from system backup. Apple may retrieve an explicitly selected iCloud photo before delivering it to the app. This adds local storage, not a new remote collection path.

New photos captured inside a recovered workspace belong to that independently opened physical SQLite workspace. A v1.2 owner attachment can describe the original capture UUID alongside its unchanged logical archive provenance; the imported description grants no file or cleanup access. A separate bounded, digest-checked local journal binds the recovery-copy ID, store kind and actual physical store ID. Cleanup verifies that journal against the actual SQLite store, retires that physical media/correction namespace, and then removes the isolated directory. Registry deletion cancels pending opens and waits for in-flight store users; stale rows cannot recreate the removed workspace. Removing only embedded source media retains the existing copy ID and its edited directory. Malformed journals and symbolic links fail closed; active root workspaces remain protected.

Shared placement evidence: explicit capture preserves the original authenticated shop snapshot, review time, authority epoch, actor/device binding and immutable report/reconciliation retry bytes in protected, backup-excluded local storage. Sending transmits the reviewed command's snapshot reference and occurrence time, not the private local ledger. Reporter status is minimal and remains distinct from current-shopper reconciliation data. Sign-out/account deletion and scoped household exit purge local evidence with a deletion epoch; a separately device-authenticated offline view can reopen only the retained native actor's original evidence, never current shopper authority. These reports are manually entered external-order evidence, not retailer or payment verification.

Optional pickup feedback starts off. Enabling it offers a prompt only on a closed, reported pickup. A saved response contains three codes for outcome, usual workflow and comparison with that workflow. It belongs to the pickup in the private local workspace and can travel in an explicitly exported owner recovery file. The app does not transmit it to an analytics service. Removing the response clears the current local record. Exported copies remain under their owner's control.

Separately consented household measurement transmits newly reviewed outcome, baseline and comparison response codes to the authenticated household service. It retains household/member/shop linkage, eligible week, consent identity and notice version, and consent/response timestamps. The service can match existing verified Production subscription state with current consent to assess household purchase retention. It does not import local feedback, grocery text, prices or member names into measurement. Withdrawal removes responses under that consent; account erasure removes associated measurement records. This linked collection is declared even though participation is optional. See `service/src/measurement.ts`, `service/migrations/019_optional_measurement.sql`, `ios/Features/Pickup/PickupFeedback.swift` and [the measurement contract](../service/API-MEASUREMENT.md). Future child-verification provider collection remains subject to provider configuration and privacy review before enablement; no unverified provider data categories are asserted here.

Local handoffs store a recipient label only when the shopper marks a specific saved cook note or retailer snapshot as sent. Each send and separately reported acknowledgment belongs to that pickup. Removing the pickup erases its local recipient history. An owner recovery file can include these records. Recipient labels, private budgets and historical custom wording are excluded from generated change lists. A full copy with custom or private wording requires a separate review. Copy, share-sheet completion and recipient acknowledgment are distinct actions.

Family decisions start as private pickup evidence. An authorized shopper chooses the shop and reviews a copy before sending it to the household service. The copy can contain dish, product, occasion, preference and explicitly observed outcome. Paid search and retention of a preference require a current named seat. Local pending commands are protected, excluded from device backup and cleared on authority loss. Account erasure removes authored records, strips the erased person's contributions and invalidates affected retry receipts. [Household decision contract](../service/API-DECISIONS.md)

Pantry and freezer batches store the household's food label, usable quantity, observation time, reviewed food identity and optional use-first choice in the local pickup. Reservations are derived from the confirmed quantities and meal priorities. Batch details can travel in an explicitly exported owner recovery file.
