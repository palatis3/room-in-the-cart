## Bounded stock-to-purchase integration candidate

**D05 / E06 / S04–S05, sent for lead scope/implementation triage after the five findings.** The list-bound pack selection cannot credit confirmed matching stock. This is a source-proven limitation, with no fresh executed native reproduction in this read-only lane. The lead requested the complete quantity provenance before affirming another gap.

`ShoppingLedger.quantity(forLineID)` at `ShoppingLedger.swift:434–459` sums manual, meal and protected contributions, subtracting within-existing links and duplicate relationships. It has no pantry term. `LedgerCheckoutAdapter.review` at `ios/App/LedgerCheckoutEntry.swift:57,67` uses that same total both for purchase-line basis and for total meal/staple demands. Gross meal demand remaining intact after stock confirmation is correct; it is not an already-net shopping quantity.

The supported route is: create local list **12 wraps** → Shop **Review checkout** → **Continue to checkout review** → **Apply reviewed checkout copy**. This creates a pickup with basket line identity L and planned demand 12. Return to ingredient L → **Confirm pantry or freezer food** (the entry exists because the matching pickup/basket line exists) → explicitly matched ingredientID L → confirm batch **8**. `PickupNewStockSection` passes that exact `ingredientID` as the lot's foodID. `saveStockLot` changes only the pickup stock lot and revision; `physicalStockNeeds` still demands 12, so its current allocation has 8 supplied / 4 short. Nothing in this path reduces the ledger's source contributions.

Return to the same ingredient → **Choose whole packs for this item**. `ios/Features/Ledger/LedgerPackReview.swift:23` constructs a request for the full ledger need with no `confirmedAtHome`. `ios/Packages/CoreDomain/Sources/CoreDomain/LedgerPackSelection.swift:28,47` deliberately rejects a nonnil stock quantity; `LedgerPackChoiceView:50` explicitly says this purchase choice does not claim pantry/freezer food. At **8 per pack**, this review yields **2 packs**, and applying that selection through `LedgerCheckoutAdapter` supplies a purchased amount of **16**, while preserving the correct gross demand 12. The separate account-free comparison correctly returns one pack when entered stock is 8, but its stock-aware answer cannot be persisted as the list-bound selection. This is not a failure of the existing no-stock arithmetic.

If affirmed against the full product scope, the bounded implementation requirement is to connect explicitly reviewed matching usable supply to the purchase selection without editing original meal demand. D05 requires subtracting confirmed usable supply; S04 combines available stock and package review. At minimum the selection needs source pickup/workspace identity, relevant stock observations and exact usable quantity, matching food/units, competing reservations, and invalidation when any of those change. Preserve the deliberate nil guard until that source proof exists. A simple scalar copied from another screen would allow stale or twice-reserved stock.

Two safe design options for the lead: (a) persist an immutable stock evidence lease with the selection and revalidate both local stores at checkout review/apply, or (b) colocate the authoritative purchase preview and confirmed allocation revision in the pickup transaction, retaining the ledger selection as observation-only until that explicit review. Both require a truthful zero-purchase representation when stock covers all demand; the present selection validation requires purchased packs > 0. Tests should follow the actual route above, then reduce/reassign stock, preserve named lunch demand, reject stale reviews, and verify that archived files confer no live stock authority. Unknown conversion must stay unresolved.
