# Dated shelf photo pilot

During the two-trip shopper study, invite six to eight outside households using English or US Spanish to try a shelf photo if they wish. Run this part on an iPhone. Record the iOS version, language, display settings and shopping location. Use an observation sheet with one row per household and trip. The two-trip responses and observations are still pending.

## At home before the first trip

Let the shopper choose one storage location and the foods they expect to shop for. With permission, record the actual amounts separately for scoring. The shopper can keep an ordinary photo in Camera Roll, record quantities in Pantry, and save a dated copy through **Pantry → Pantry tools → Shelf photos**. For a fair comparison, offer the same image through the Photos picker. Also try a new camera photo on a consenting device. Record setup time, taps, any denied permission and whether the shopper completes each path independently. Leave household photos on their phone.

## In the aisle

On the first trip, ask the shopper to decide whether to buy a named item. In separate trials, let them inspect the saved shelf photo under **Shop in store → List options → Shelf photos**, read the recorded amount, or open Camera Roll. Rotate the order across households. Ask when the evidence was captured and what the shopper can tell about the quantity. Record time, taps, the purchase decision, any incorrect amount and whether the shopper identifies uncertainty.

About a week later, ask the shopper to make a comparable decision with their own phone, without naming any of the three routes. Record their first choice, any switch and why, and whether they voluntarily use or update the shelf photo. Ask whether the storage location changed between the two trips. Verify the decision against the household's actual stock with permission. Keep unknown stock or an unobserved result marked unknown.

## Checks before reporting

- Open a dated image, inspect a package, and cancel choosing a replacement. Reopen from the active shopping list. Check the date and image against the first trip.
- Replace the photo, then delete it. Reopen after leaving the app. Verify the photo for another storage location is still present and the chosen Camera Roll original is still available.
- Switch private adult or workspace while the editor is open, then return to Shop. Verify another adult cannot see or change the photo. Delete the original pantry through Records and verify its shelf photos disappear.
- Repeat the ordinary inspection with VoiceOver and accessibility-size text on the minimum and current supported iOS. Record camera permission handling and any missing or obstructed actions.

Summarize observed repeat-use choices, correct decisions, elapsed time and errors by route and language. Include the number of households that declined or could not complete each route. Use the observations to decide whether the shortcut merits more prominent placement.
