# What survived fifteen generations

The audit starts with the original briefs, follows the Gen 10 register into the Gen 11–14 journeys, and reconciles it against Gen 15. It distinguishes a product decision from a competitor's advertised feature, an experimental prototype control, and a research recruitment or pricing target. Each row below names an observable behavior a builder could otherwise miss. Original paths and content fingerprints are in the [source manifest](references/sources.json). Current instructions are in the [feature contracts](02-features.md).

## Reopened decisions

| Historical boundary | Why it existed | Current first-release treatment |
|---|---|---|
| X01 · Marketplace, checkout, delivery | Live stock, ordering, payments and fulfillment belong to the provider. | Prepare a retailer-specific dated list that the shopper copies/shares and can open externally. The next integration may add authorized cart writes after rights and confirmation tests. Operating a marketplace is not a planner feature. |
| X02 · Universal deals and routing | Sticker prices omit stock, fees, extra trips and the shopper's time. | **A03** lets the shopper enter two explicit alternatives and compare equivalent commitments, totals, travel cost and time. No market-wide price or stock claim; choosing an option never places orders or schedules a route. |
| X03 · Medical/allergy decisions | A scan, translation or similar-looking product cannot certify suitability. | Household-entered exclusions are hard constraints. Mark unknown labels for household review; never mark food medically safe. |
| X04 · Unrestricted generated recipes or diet coaching | A plausible recipe can invent ingredients, yields or acceptable substitutions. | **A02** creates editable dinner proposals from supported templates or household-approved patterns. The adult reviews every ingredient, yield, quantity and exclusion before accepting; scheduling is a second action. No dietary targets or medical advice. |
| X05 · Social feed, school calendars and chores | A family grocery request must stay private; full general scheduling is a different service. | **A01** gives authorized adults the meal week, guests, away nights, portions and lunches. It has no public profile, social feed, external calendar scraping or chores. |
| X06 · Mandatory scan or credential collection | Camera denial and missing receipts must never block the next decision; a retailer login is not the app's credential. | Ship optional photo, OCR, barcode and receipt review plus a typed alternative. No retailer password storage, scraping or unattended checkout. |

**E05 is the later release.** Keep an adapter interface with capability per retailer/region/store, explicit shopper consent, idempotency, expiry, confirmed response and a portable fallback. No provider token or partner approval is needed for release one. Trial Kroger as a possible future authorized cart add, with Walmart gated on partner rights. A product-link open, local cart mock or estimate can never count as a confirmed provider write. See the original retailer feasibility study in the manifest.

**Free versus Household.** Free covers private offline use, manual meals, one reusable dinner, family requests, basic budget preview, manual edits and repair, manual quotes, manual calendar planning, and storage/recovery. Make the new dinner-idea draft and adult ingredient review accessible on Free. Household adds assisted multi-meal reuse/rotation, pairings, guest/budget scenarios, history tools and change comparisons for two named adults. Price assumption remains $49.99/year in the US; use actual localized StoreKit product data in the purchase UI. Approving a request and reading/exporting one's data never depend on payment.

**Experiments are not dependencies.** Include the discussed data and UI seams: compact rows/whole-row checking with persistent restore, opt-in on-device aliases, scoped iPhone quick entry, large print and device language. Preserve the baseline with default Apple keyboard Dictation and the family's existing reminders or smart-speaker list via consented text import. A comparison to Alexa, a rival's meal generator or a third-party Android app is research evidence rather than a promise of that vendor's native integration. Mixed-phone contribution and cook views ship on web. US English and US Spanish ship; other countries use a capability matrix and require their own fixtures before a listing claim.
