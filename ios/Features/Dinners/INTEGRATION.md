# Dinner feature integration

`DinnerPlanningHome(ledger:access:phase:ideaCatalog:runtimeChanges:)` is the app entry. The parent composition supplies current runtime authorization and purchase phase; the feature never decodes authorization or paid access from a restored workspace. The optional publisher refreshes visible permission and entitlement state; every command independently reads the provider again. `LedgerModel` conforms to `DinnerWorkspaceStore` in a new-file extension. All persistence goes through its existing atomic `change` boundary.

Manual dinners, explicit quantities, calendar moves, day notes, protected food, editable ideas and the selected Free reusable dinner work without paid tools. The accepted library is independent of chosen occurrences. An idea is reviewed, then accepted; choosing it is a separate preview and apply. Legacy `mealTemplates` remain visible for reviewed adoption, preserving their IDs and versions. All chosen dinners includes earlier dates and hidden occurrences without recreating needs.

Guest automation requires reviewed ingredient/group bindings. The integration method is `previewGuests(occurrenceID:servings:isolated:)`, where values are reviewed totals, not increments. The new `DinnerPreviewView` shows the result. Protected groups do not scale automatically. Free exploration is isolated and cannot apply; manual quantities remain available.

Rotation choices, calendar moves, guest changes, protected reservations and store adoption use immutable domain previews. Apply reads current authority, phase and revisions again; the ledger store supplies durable CAS and exact retry behavior. Shared Apply is always disabled in this local adapter, even if a supplied context sets `sharedCommitAuthorized`. Saving a proposal is explicitly described as local and unsent. A frozen or reported-placed basket requires an amendment for grocery changes.

The two-store editor captures the same commitments, inclusive retailer totals, confirmation of included charges, branch/mode/slot/source/time, availability and quantity review, incremental trip cash and time. Unknown amounts remain unknown. Save reviewed comparison precedes store-adoption preview; changing the shop basis invalidates the active quote. It never combines charges with an already inclusive total.

The scenario evidence editor requires explicit current/candidate recipe choices and ingredient-to-food identities. `DinnerDemand.ingredientID` comes from the accepted template; names never create a match. Requested portions scale exact reviewed recipe quantities in the draft builder. Protected-group recipes require their original yield or a separately accepted manual version. Confirmed stock requires review of outside reservations and is allocated once. Usable pack yield, price, availability and maximum pack count are entered independently. Unchanged basket costs are shared unchanged between scenarios; missing costs, yields, timing, unavailable packs or incomplete recipe coverage cannot establish a complete result.

Scenario comparisons are read-only. The separate candidate-dinner action chooses the accepted recipe's saved servings, after a fresh grocery preview; it does not apply scenario serving changes, consume stock, create pack selections or attach a checkout quote. A future atomic scenario-adoption integration needs persisted evidence and stock/pack allocations. No server/shared commit, live price feed, catalog feed, allergen certification, or production stock synchronization is implied. The app passes an empty idea catalog until it has an explicitly reviewed source; manual ideas remain usable.

Current fixed-charge scenario input does not model retailer threshold discounts or nonlinear fees. Those require reviewed inclusive quote evidence through the two-store form. Cross-dimension usable conversions remain unresolved unless supplied in reviewed recipe evidence; the UI does not infer edible yields, densities, peeling, bones or cooking losses.

All feature strings are in `Dinners.xcstrings` with English and Spanish values. Forms use native labels, scalable text, explicit units, grouped VoiceOver summaries, and focused error messages. The app owner performs simulator, device VoiceOver, contrast, StoreKit and acceptance runs; this feature's isolated checks do not substitute for those.

`ShopBasis.cap` is the active amount available for its declared scope. A separately recorded fixed reserve is displayed but is not subtracted again. For example, the foundation's active dinner cap is already after its fixed reserve. A dinner-only or selected-item cap never establishes whole-pickup assurance. A future budget envelope may derive an active cap from total funds and allocations explicitly; it must not silently reinterpret existing saved caps.
