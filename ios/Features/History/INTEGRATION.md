# E09 native price records and period planning

Foundation 2.1 E09 entry: **Records → Prices and period budgets**. The native session factory lives in `HouseholdModel`; the navigation wrapper retains one feature coordinator. Recovered files and the private local ledger do not supply household authority or upload automatically.

The editor preserves exact/bounded/unknown USD evidence, original pack text and optional rational usable quantity, mass/fluid/count unit, store, branch, channel, fulfillment mode, eligibility, observed timestamp and source locale/text. History filters apply to loaded authorized pages; pagination remains explicit. Free reopening uses saved observation references, with live source authorization on each read. Saved period discovery and raw records remain separate from paid analysis.

Periods are explicitly reviewed shop-plan envelopes. Reserve amounts already excluded from the entered budget are explanatory. Future reserved plans reduce planning money; applying a reservation assigns it to a shop without also retaining a reserved amount. Released/applied evidence is immutable. Server summaries separate posted spending, expected credits, included credits, payment holds, expected unpaid and projected commitment. Unknown upper bounds keep minimum remaining unknown. Partial scope responses expose only the permitted subtotal, never cached envelope funding/title/reservations or a hidden-source count.

All writes require a complete review and separate approval. The client validates inputs, refreshes current authority and period revision, then the coordinator persists a bound exact-byte intent before sending. An uncertain result retries that original operation. Exact receipt replay does not silently rebase the plan. A stale result can be explicitly reviewed against current records before clearing the local retry; this does not undo a service mutation.

The retry store uses complete iOS file protection, 0600 permissions, backup exclusion and actor-atomic writes. A deletion epoch rejects late saves. Sign-out, access loss and the account-deletion sign-out path purge the store. Screen projections are invalidated on lock, background, identity, household, selected shop, role and capability changes. Paid `membership_inactive` responses preserve the enrolled session so Free raw-record routes can still operate; other authorization denials retain the established failure behavior.

The local migration 012 service is the authority for paid seats, adult/current-shopper ownership, source versions, exact idempotency, period ownership and financial math. This slice does not change global `X-Shop-ID` selection or the backend. Human Spanish, VoiceOver/Dynamic Type and live native-to-local-service acceptance remain distinct from structural localization, model/transport fixtures and native compilation.
